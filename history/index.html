Kips Bay
Towers

Designed by Pritzker Prize-winning architect I.M. Pei, this complex is a Brutalist masterpiece in the heart of Murray Hill.

1960 Completion Date
"Pei transformed initial six-building plans into two towers to prioritize open park space, choosing 50 sapling trees over a planned Picasso sculpture."

The Pei Vision

Occupying a serene, private park-like enclave, the towers' hallmark floor-to-ceiling picture windows pioneered modern development design trends.

Unique Site Features

Spanning 7.5 acres including 3 acres of private landscaped gardens, a playground, basketball/pickleball court, and sports field.

Historical Context

The site once held the original Phipps Houses (1906). By the 1950s, these tenements faced condemnation, enabling this modern transformation.

Development Story

Originally federally financed as 'Kips Bay Plaza', Pei reluctantly took the project under developer William Zeckendorf.

Brutalist Specs

  • Units: 1,118 Residences
  • Conversion: Nov. 1981
  • Floors: 20 Stories per tower

Modern Amenities
