Final
Exit

"Take a moment to look around before you go. It takes only a sec, yet it matters more than you know. For the one who comes next or for you when you're back, a little effort now keeps everything on track."

01 Mandatory Actions

  • HVAC UNITS Ensure all heating/cooling units are turned OFF.
  • ELECTRONICS Unplug all TV power cords from electrical outlets.
  • WINDOWS & BLINDS Lock all windows. Position blinds exactly halfway.
  • LIGHTING Power down all interior lights.

02 Turnover Details

Kitchen & Bath: Wipe down surfaces with disinfecting wipes (found under the kitchen sink).

Waste: Empty all trash cans. Replace with new bags found at the bottom of each bin.

Linens: Place used towels/sheets in the laundry hamper (Main Bedroom closet). Replacements are in the hall closet.

Fixtures: Close bathtub plugs; lift all toilet lids and seats.

03 Final Handover

Leave the keys in the foyer table drawer.

The door will lock automatically behind you. Ensure you have your phone!
